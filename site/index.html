Meet the data

Each domain is its own schema. All four share a community_districts table with the 59 NYC community districts (codes like BK05, a name, the borough, and population), which you'll use for per-capita rates and maps. Every table and column has a description in Unity Catalog. Genie reads those descriptions, which is the first reason a well-documented table gets better answers.

3 Round 1: ask it raw

Goal: learn where an uncurated space is reliable and where it gives answers that look right and are wrong. Ask each question in your space, read the SQL, and compare it to the expected answer. 10:40 · 15 min

Here's the thing about Genie: it almost always answers, and it answers confidently. Usually it's right, even on raw tables. The risk is a confident, plausible, wrong number on an agency term or rule it can't know. Round 1 is about learning to spot those. Round 2 fixes them.

In the lab: ask 3 or 4 questions from your dataset's tab, read the SQL, then open Expected answer. Do the rest in the open lab.

✅ Raw Genie got it right in our test run ❌ Raw Genie was wrong confidently Agency definition needs knowledge that isn't in the data SQL math snapshots, ratios, latest-record

Genie isn't deterministic: your run may differ from ours, which is exactly why you read the SQL.

How many older adults are waiting for home care?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong 5,630: added up every program's waitlist.

Agency definition aging_programs has a waitlist for every program. "Home care" means the EISEP Home Care waitlist only. Adding meals and case management waitlists overstates the backlog.

Expected answer

… on the EISEP Home Care waitlist. Summing every program gives …. In testing, both a raw space and a lightly curated one made that mistake until a one-line rule was added.

Which community districts have the most isolated frail older adults?

✅ Raw Genie got it right SI01 North Shore on top, via the pre-built roll-up table.

Agency definition "Isolated" isn't a column, so Genie has to guess. Agency definition: medically_frail AND living_alone. Check what it picked.

Expected answer

Top district: ….

What did home care cost this month?

✅ Raw Genie got it right $19,152,628: used the latest month in the data.

Agency definition On the calendar, "this month" is October 2026, but the latest data is September 2026. Does Genie return nothing, ask, or use the latest month? Which does your agency want?

Expected answer

September 2026 total: ….

What share of clients meet the minimum ADL need for personal care?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong 65.3%: counted clients who met it on any assessment, not their latest.

SQL math Clients have several assessments, and frailer clients are reassessed more often. Counting every row over-states need. Look for ROW_NUMBER() … = 1 or QUALIFY in the SQL.

Expected answer

Latest assessment per client: … of … clients. Across all assessment rows: ….

Which agency has the worst farebox recovery?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong Staten Island Ferry, 0.0.

Agency definition The Staten Island Ferry is fare-free by policy, so its ratio is always 0. That's technically correct and tells you nothing. Agency rule: exclude SIF unless asked.

Expected answer

Excluding SIF: ….

How much has congestion pricing contributed to the operating budget?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong $932.6M, counted as operating funding.

Agency definition CBD toll revenue is lockboxed for the capital program. The right answer is "none".

Expected answer

$0 to operating. Net revenue to capital since launch: ….

How much did the buses lose in 2025?

✅ Raw Genie got it right −$66.5M, matching both bus agencies by mode.

Agency definition "The buses" = NYC Transit Bus and MTA Bus Company. Check whether both made it into the SQL, and how they were matched (by ID or by a fuzzy ILIKE '%bus%').

What was the MTA's farebox recovery ratio in 2025?

✅ Raw Genie got it right 0.265, weighted correctly.

SQL math AVG(farebox_recovery_ratio) weights the Staten Island Railway the same as the subway. Look for SUM(fare)/SUM(expense).

Expected answer

Weighted: …. Naive average of the ratio: ….

How many delinquent accounts are there?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong 582: counted only Lien-Eligible accounts.

Agency definition Is "delinquent" a collections status or any account with a balance? Agency definition: arrears > 0 in the latest bill month.

Expected answer

… accounts with a balance in Sep 2026.

How many water main breaks were there last winter?

✅ Raw Genie got it right 172 (in another run it asked which months count as winter).

Agency definition In testing, a raw space asked which months count as winter. That's good behavior. Answer it, then turn your answer into an instruction so nobody has to answer it again.

Expected answer

Dec 2025–Feb 2026: … breaks.

What is the total arrears balance by borough?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong Summed the running balance across all 15 months: about 9× too high.

SQL math arrears_balance_usd is a running balance. Summing it across 15 months badly inflates it. Look for a filter on the latest bill_month.

Expected answer

Citywide as of Sep 2026: …. Summed across all months: …, …× too high.

What were total water and sewer charges in FY2026?

✅ Raw Genie got it right Used fiscal_year = 2026.

SQL math NYC FY2026 = July 2025–June 2026. Look for fiscal_year = 2026, not YEAR(bill_month) = 2026.

Expected answer

Total billed FY2026: ….

How full were the reservoirs at the worst point of the 2024 drought?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong Reported each system separately and never gave a system-wide figure.

SQL math Averaging pct_full gives the small Croton system the same weight as Delaware. System-wide = SUM(storage_bg)/SUM(capacity_bg).

Expected answer

Low point: ….

Which neighborhoods have the best restaurant inspection scores?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong BX02 Hunts Point: averaged re-inspections in too.

Agency definition Inspection scores are penalty points: lower is better. Agency practice: compare places on initial inspections only, because only poor performers get re-inspected. Check the sort order and the filter.

Expected answer

Best: …. Worst: ….

How many restaurants did DOHMH shut down in 2025?

✅ Raw Genie got it right 214, using the closure action.

Agency definition A closure is an action on the inspection ("Establishment Closed by DOHMH"). A C grade is not a closure. Also check what got counted: restaurants or closure events? Some restaurants were closed twice.

Expected answer

… restaurants closed in 2025 (… closure events). Counting C grades instead gives …. In testing, our first curated space answered with closure events. A separate Restaurants Closed measure fixed it.

What share of rodent inspections found rats in 2025?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong 16.5%: divided by every visit, including bait and clean-up.

Agency definition Bait and Clean Up visits are treatments, not inspections, so they can't "find" rats. Agency definition: Rat Activity ÷ Initial inspections.

Expected answer

… of Initial inspections. Dividing by every visit gives ….

How many restaurants currently have an A grade?

❌ Raw Genie was wrong 4,538: used each restaurant's latest inspection even when it had no grade.

SQL math Restaurants are inspected many times. Current grade = the grade on each restaurant's latest A/B/C inspection. Look for ROW_NUMBER() or QUALIFY, not a count of A rows.

Expected answer

… restaurants (…). Counting A-grade inspections gives …. Watch the order of operations, too: in testing, a curated space filtered to A rows before taking each restaurant's latest grade and got about 5,700. The fix was an example query for exactly this question.

What is the average restaurant inspection score?

✅ Raw Genie got it right 15.5, straight from the inspections table.

SQL math If the SQL joins violations, every inspection is repeated once per violation and dirtier inspections count more. Average from inspections alone.

Expected answer

… across all inspections, or … on initial inspections only (the agency's basis for comparisons). Either is defensible if the answer says which. With the violations join: …, which is wrong.

The curation toolbox, from lightest to heaviest

ToolWhereUse it for
Column descriptions & synonymsConfigure → Sources → table → ✎ on a columnUnits, local jargon ("debt" = arrears, "riders" = trips), hiding columns nobody should use.
Text instructionsConfigure → TextBusiness definitions, default time windows, "always / never" rules, how to present answers.
JoinsConfigure → Examples → Add → JoinsTelling Genie how tables relate and the cardinality (many-to-one, etc.).
SQL expressions (measures, filters, dimensions)Configure → Examples → Add → Measure / Filter / FieldReusable calculations: farebox recovery, system-wide % full, a winter filter.
Example SQL queriesConfigure → Examples → Add (SQL query)Fully worked answers to tricky question patterns. The strongest signal, so save it for the hard ones.
Metric viewsCreated by the setup notebook (or by you in the SQL editor), then Configure → Sources → AddGoverned KPIs defined once in Unity Catalog (YAML): measures, dimensions, synonyms, joins. Reused by every Genie space, dashboard and SQL query. The durable home for definitions.
Trusted assets / functionsConfigure → Examples: a query with Add parameter, or a UC SQL functionQuestions leadership asks every week, where the answer must be exact and repeatable. Genie labels answers from these as Trusted, so users can tell a reviewed answer from a generated one.
Rule of thumb: fix it in Unity Catalog metadata first (it helps every tool, not just this space), then text instructions, then SQL expressions, and use example SQL for the patterns that still fail. Keep instructions short and specific. A wall of prose makes Genie worse.

SQL expressions

Live inside one Genie space. Fast to add, and great for trying out a definition. Nobody else benefits: the dashboard team and the next space re-invent the same farebox formula.

Metric views

Live in Unity Catalog, governed like a table. Define "Farebox Recovery" or "Current Arrears" once, with synonyms and comments, and every Genie space, AI/BI dashboard and SQL user gets the same number.

How the traps map: each domain's metric view encodes its definition traps as measures: Isolated Frail Clients (frail AND living alone), Farebox Recovery Ratio (weighted, excludes the fare-free ferry), Service Group ("the buses" = NYCT Bus + MTA Bus), Current Arrears and Delinquent Accounts (latest-month snapshot), Closures and Average Initial Score (DOHMH). Measures compose, so a ratio stays correct at any grain.

Cheat sheet

Asking well

  • Name the metric, the slice, and the time: arrears by borough as of September 2026.
  • Use follow-ups to refine instead of re-asking.
  • Start a new chat when you change topics.
  • Ask Genie to explain or show the SQL.

Curating well

  • Promote definitions that more than one space or dashboard needs into a metric view.
  • Under 10 tables per space; one business domain.
  • Define every term your users say that isn't a column name.
  • Put units in column descriptions.
  • Use example SQL for patterns (latest-snapshot, weighted ratio, fiscal year), not for one-off questions.
  • Re-run benchmarks after every change.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

"Run all" is greyed out, or nothing happens

Connect the notebook first: top right → Connect → Serverless. Free Edition only has serverless compute.

The first cell fails with a permission error creating a schema

The notebook couldn't use your default catalog. Open Catalog in the sidebar, find a catalog you own (Free Edition: workspace), type its name into the catalog widget at the top of the notebook, and run all again.

"No SQL warehouse found" or Genie says the warehouse is stopped

Open SQL Warehouses in the sidebar and start the Serverless Starter Warehouse. Genie also wakes it automatically; the first query just takes a few extra seconds.

A notebook printed "Could not reach the Genie API"

That's fine. Your data and metric views are built, but the ready-made spaces weren't. Build your own space in Lab 1, and for Lab 5 compare against the answer key in the Round 2 tabs.

Notebook 2 says "Run the genie_byoq_nyc_workshop notebook first"

Notebook 2 only adds Genie spaces on top of the data. Run notebook 1 to the end first (look for the ✓ lines), then run notebook 2 again.

Compute shut down / quota exceeded

Free Edition has a daily usage quota. Your tables and spaces are kept; compute comes back the next day. Pair up with a neighbor in the meantime.

My instruction changes don't seem to work

Start a new chat. Existing conversations keep their earlier context. Also check that your instruction doesn't contradict an example SQL query.

My numbers don't match the expected answers

The notebook uses fixed random seeds, so every workspace gets identical data. If yours differ, check the SQL Genie wrote. That's usually the lesson.

Does Genie send our data to a model provider?

Genie sends metadata (table/column names and descriptions, instructions) and your question to the model to write SQL. The SQL runs on your warehouse under your permissions.
