Set up your free Databricks workspace

For the hands-on lab at NYC Databricks Day (Tue, Oct 27, 9:00 AM – 12:30 PM ET, Slalom offices, New York) you'll build in your own free Databricks workspace. It's Databricks Free Edition: no cost, no cloud account, no credit card. Please do this before you arrive so we can start building on time.

This is a Bring Your Own Questions workshop. Come with 2–3 real questions your office gets asked about its data (e.g. "Which neighborhoods have the longest waits for …?"). You'll try them in Genie during the lab and leave with a list to take back to your agency.

Five steps

  1. Open the sign-up pageGo to databricks.com/learn/free-edition and choose to sign up.
  2. Choose how you sign inA Google account is fastest (one click). You can also use Microsoft, or any email address, which gets a one-time passcode.
  3. Verify your accountApprove the Google or Microsoft prompt, or type in the passcode emailed to you. Free Edition doesn't use your agency's single sign-on.
  4. Let Databricks build your workspaceIt's created for you automatically. There are no clusters to size and no settings to pick.
  5. You land in your workspaceThat's it. Remember which login you used, because you'll sign back in with it on the day.

Two quick checks

If either check fails, don't worry: come at 8:30 when doors open and a Databricks or Slalom coach will sort it out with you.

Good to know

Which email to use

Many government email systems block outside one-time passcodes. If yours doesn't arrive in a few minutes, use a personal Gmail instead. It also gives you one-click sign-in.

Nothing else to install

Everything runs in your browser. On the day you'll import one ready-made notebook that builds the workshop data in about 3 minutes.

One workspace, yours to keep

Each account gets one workspace. It stays after the event, so you can keep experimenting. Free Edition is for learning and non-commercial use.

What to bring

Your laptop, and your 2–3 questions (in your head or on your phone is fine). You'll write them into the guide at the start of the day.

On the day: open the workshop guide at workshopsdbx.github.io/genie. Everything else is in there.
